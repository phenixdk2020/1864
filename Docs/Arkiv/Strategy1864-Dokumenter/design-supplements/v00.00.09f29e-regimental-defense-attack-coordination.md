# PROJECT 1864 — v00.00.09f29e Regimental Defense, Objective Visuals & Coordinated Attack

## Status

**Prototype:** v00.00.09f29e TEST  
**Design baseline:** v00.02.13  
**Scope:** Oberstløjtnant HUD/visuals, defensive frontage, precise destination arrival, coordinated multi-company attacks and enlarged-map navigation hardening.

## Problem statement

Runtime QA on v00.00.09f29c exposed six related command/readability/movement problems:

1. The Oberstløjtnant still used the legacy F28 HUD while Major and Company already used the F29C compact red/green HUD language.
2. A placed `FORSVAR HER` / `ANGRIB HER` objective disappeared after the click, making the commander's intent difficult to read when zoomed out.
3. Selecting the Oberstløjtnant showed only Oberstløjtnant → Major links. Major → Company links and company final destination footprints were hidden.
4. The regimental `FORSVAR HER` split the two battalion objectives too far apart, leaving a corridor through the exact point the player ordered the regiment to defend.
5. Several companies ordered to attack the same enemy company converged on one centre line and collapsed into an unreadable crowd instead of deploying around the target.
6. The F29B battlefield had been enlarged to 5760 × 3840 m, but the active V4 navigation layer still used the old ±176 × ±116 m prototype grid. Valid units on the enlarged map could therefore be treated as outside navigation bounds.

The QA video that triggered this revision was still running the visible marker `v00.00.09f29c TEST`; therefore the semantic-zoom F29D layer had not yet been present in that recording. F29E retains F29D and adds command/combat/navigation hardening on top.

---

## 1. Unified Oberstløjtnant HUD

The Regimental HQ now uses the same 90 px compact bottom-command language introduced for Major and Company in F29C.

### Sections

- **ENHEDSINFO / AI**
  - current / initial men
  - losses
  - morale
  - cohesion
  - average ammunition
  - AI ON/OFF
  - DEF / BAL / OFF doctrine
- **REGIMENTSORDRER**
  - ANGRIB HER
  - FORSVAR HER
  - RYK FREM
  - TILBAGETRÆK
  - SAML
  - STOP / HOLD
- **BATALJONER UNDER OBERSTLØJTNANT**
  - Major A / Major B
  - men / losses / morale / ammunition
  - battalion AI and doctrine state

State colours follow the same rule as F29C:

- **green = active / selected**
- **red = inactive / not selected**

The legacy F28 HUD remains in code as a dependency, but the new HUD draws above it and consumes the already-reserved bottom-panel input area so hidden legacy buttons cannot double-fire.

---

## 2. Persistent regimental objective marker

The pre-click target cursor from F29A remains, but after the command is committed the selected Oberstløjtnant now retains a persistent objective marker.

The marker contains:

- terrain-following circle;
- centre cross;
- order label in screen space;
- order-specific colour;
- larger radius for `FORSVAR HER` than ordinary movement orders.

First QA radii:

| Order | Radius |
|---|---:|
| FORSVAR HER | 72 m |
| ANGRIB HER | 58 m |
| SAML | 40 m |
| RYK FREM / TILBAGETRÆK | 50 m |

The marker is only persistent while the Regimental HQ is selected. This keeps the battlefield clean while making commander intent explicit during inspection.

---

## 3. Full command-chain visibility from the Oberstløjtnant

Selecting the Oberstløjtnant must expose the whole direct hierarchy currently controlled by that HQ:

`Oberstløjtnant → Major A / Major B → 1.-8. Kompagni`

F29E therefore forces the existing terrain-following Major → Company relationship lines visible while the Regimental HQ is selected. The existing Oberstløjtnant → Major links remain visible through the F29A hierarchy layer.

This is a relationship overlay, not an order route.

---

## 4. Company final destinations at Regimental HQ level

While the Oberstløjtnant is selected, all active company mission footprints are visible regardless of which Major owns the company.

For companies still moving, the route to the assigned footprint is also shown. After arrival, the footprint remains visible while the route disappears.

This allows the player to inspect the Regimental order decomposition:

`Regimental objective → battalion objectives → company slots`

without clicking each Major separately.

---

## 5. Precise slot arrival

F27 historically considered a company arrived at approximately 4.5 m from the mission centre. A company destination footprint is only approximately 6.4 m deep. This allowed the route line to disappear while the company centre was visibly still outside the displayed rectangle.

F29E adds a narrow correction pass:

- it only activates inside the legacy 5 m arrival window;
- it does not override an F26 under-fire reaction;
- it temporarily completes the last metres of the existing mission;
- final acceptance is approximately **0.85 m** from the mission centre;
- after precise arrival, local Officer AI resumes.

The correction does not replace normal route/navigation logic and does not teleport formations.

---

## 6. Continuous `FORSVAR HER` frontage

The old Regimental `FORSVAR HER` used the generic battalion lateral offset of approximately 170 m to each side of the clicked point. With battalion-level company spacing and reserves this could leave the objective itself largely undefended.

F29E defines `FORSVAR HER` as an anchored defensive frontage:

- clicked point = centre of the regimental defensive line;
- nearest known enemy determines the defensive facing when possible;
- the two battalion centres are initially placed approximately **84 m** to either side of the centre;
- each four-company battalion may still retain a local reserve under its existing F27 logic;
- with three front companies per battalion at 60 m spacing, the inner company footprints meet at the regimental centre instead of leaving an open corridor.

This is intentionally different from `RYK FREM` / generic two-battalion side-by-side spacing. A defensive objective is an area that must actually be denied to the enemy.

Terrain/river slot safety remains authoritative and may adjust individual company positions where the nominal line is physically illegal.

---

## 7. Regimental attack role correction

The implementation still contained the original F28 total-travel permutation even though the intended F29B design rule had already been documented.

F29E makes the documented rule executable:

- for `FRONT + RESERVE`, the battalion nearest the objective is FRONT;
- for `FRONT + FLANK`, the battalion nearest the objective is FRONT;
- the other battalion receives RESERVE or FLANK respectively;
- side-by-side dispositions may still minimize crossing distance.

An already-forward battalion must therefore not march backwards merely because the rear slot is geometrically closer to its Major HQ.

---

## 8. Coordinated multi-company attack

F29B correctly stopped the old frontage planner from issuing competing physical movement orders. The remaining problem was that several `AttackTarget` companies could then independently calculate a direct closing vector to the same target centre.

F29E adds a coordination layer that preserves the single-movement-owner principle:

- physical movement remains issued through each company's existing `OfficerAIController`;
- a disabled controller means Major/higher-command authority and is never overridden;
- F26 under-fire reaction suspends coordination locally;
- only explicit `AttackTarget` groups are coordinated;
- sticky-target behaviour from F29B remains authoritative;
- coordinated destinations are passed through the shared F23 formation-footprint safety logic and therefore use the real enlarged battlefield bounds.

### Deployment

For 2-4 companies attacking one target, companies are ordered by their current left/right position and receive distinct positions on a frontal arc around the target. This reduces crossing and prevents all attackers from collapsing onto one point.

For larger groups, the first four form the assault frontage and additional companies receive deeper support positions.

At an assigned slot the company holds its tactical position, faces the designated target and can fire through the normal Regiment fire system. Material target/slot movement can trigger replanning while small changes are ignored to prevent continuous slot-chasing.

The first implementation is deliberately conservative: it is a formation/deconfliction layer, not a complete assault doctrine or replacement for future brigade/battalion attack planning.

## 9. Wide-battlefield navigation authority

F29B enlarged the tactical battlefield to **5760 × 3840 m**, but the older V4 navigation code still had the historical prototype constants `BattlefieldHalfWidth = 176` and `BattlefieldHalfDepth = 116`. Its recovery and steering code could therefore clamp perfectly legal units and destinations into the old tiny central grid.

F29E treats this as an authority bug rather than a tuning issue. A new wide-map navigation guard:

- disables the obsolete V4/V3/V1 bounded movement writers and their old recovery layer;
- uses `PrototypeBootstrap.BattlefieldHalfWidth/HalfDepth` as the authoritative current map bounds;
- preserves the true final goal while issuing only temporary obstacle-detour steering;
- uses lightweight local tree/building/fence detours rather than a global 4 m A* grid over the full 5760 × 3840 m map;
- never interprets the F15 bridge phase-machine's temporary steering points as new player/HQ final goals;
- leaves **river crossing exclusively to `PrototypeRiverBridgeOnly09F3`**, which already uses the current `PrototypeBootstrap.StreamCenterX()` geometry and fixed bridge phases.

This avoids a global million-cell A* expansion while removing the catastrophic old-map clamp. A later navigation milestone may replace the lightweight detour system with hierarchical/path-sector navigation appropriate for very large battles.

---

## 10. Semantic zoom compatibility

F29E is designed to remain readable in the F29D semantic zoom system:

- persistent objective markers use `LineRenderer`, so they remain in Strategic view;
- company destination footprints and command links remain visible when 3D meshes are hidden;
- the new Oberstløjtnant HUD is screen-space and independent of 3D unit rendering;
- NATO counters remain the long-range unit/HQ representation.

Future work should make NATO HQ counters directly clickable in screen space when the 3D HQ mesh is hidden.

## QA checklist

1. Visible marker reads `PROJECT 1864 | v00.00.09f29e TEST`.
2. Selecting the Oberstløjtnant shows the new compact HUD rather than the visible legacy layout.
3. AI ON is green / AI OFF red; only the active DEF/BAL/OFF doctrine is green.
4. Place `FORSVAR HER`; keep the Oberstløjtnant selected and verify the persistent defence circle/cross remains at the clicked point.
5. With Oberstløjtnant selected, verify Oberstløjtnant → both Majors and both Majors → all eight companies are visible.
6. Verify all company destination rectangles are visible from Regimental HQ selection.
7. A company route must not disappear while the company centre is still visibly outside its destination footprint.
8. Place `FORSVAR HER` on open terrain. The centre of the clicked objective must be covered by the defensive frontage rather than left as a corridor.
9. Repeat the bridge defence QA. Terrain/river safety may alter individual slots, but the formation should still attempt to deny the ordered centre.
10. Give 2-4 companies an explicit attack on one enemy company. They should deploy to distinct attack positions rather than collapse into one blob.
11. During the same attack, trigger F26 under-fire reaction and verify coordination waits instead of fighting the reaction.
12. Give a higher Major order while coordinated attack is active; the disabled local controller must immediately yield to the higher command.
13. Move companies well outside the old ±176/±116 area and issue movement orders. They must remain on the large battlefield and must not be clamped/teleported back to the old navigation rectangle.
14. Test a long move blocked by trees/buildings and verify the wide-map obstacle detour preserves the original final goal.
15. Test a move/attack across the river and verify the existing bridge-only phase machine still owns the crossing.
16. Verify F29D semantic zoom still shows command lines/objective markers in Operational/Strategic view.
17. Regression: square, charge, forced march, river endpoint safety and F29C Major/Company HUD remain functional.
