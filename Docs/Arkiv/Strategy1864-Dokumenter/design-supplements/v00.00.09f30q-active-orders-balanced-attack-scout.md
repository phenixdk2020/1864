# v00.00.09f30q — Active Orders, Balanced Attack Front & Cavalry Scout Design

## Purpose
F30Q fixes two runtime/UI problems observed in QA and records the future cavalry reconnaissance task for the real FOG/LOS model.

## 1. Officer order button lifecycle
The shared F29G officer HUD now uses three meaningful states:

- **Red** — no active/pending order of this type.
- **Blue** — order is currently pending target placement or actively executing.
- **Green** — remains reserved for toggles/state selections such as AI, doctrine, fire policy and formation.

The rule applies to Major, Regiment, Brigade and Division.

### Mission completion semantics
- `ANGRIB HER`: active while subordinate missions are moving, after arrival while local combat/under-fire contact continues, or while a live enemy still contests the objective area.
- `FORSVAR HER`: standing order; remains active until replaced.
- `STOP / HOLD`: standing order; remains active until replaced.
- `RYK FREM`, `TILBAGETRÆK`, `SAML`: active while subordinate movement executors remain.

## 2. Balanced attack-front correction
The previous BAL regimental attack plan could create two reserve layers at once:
1. an entire battalion 285 m behind the attack front; and
2. local company reserve under each Major.

F30Q removes the whole-battalion BAL reserve.

Current attack disposition:
- **BAL** — both battalions forward side-by-side; Major-level local reserve remains possible.
- **DEF** — may retain one whole battalion in regimental reserve.
- **OFF** — may use flank-capable disposition.

This keeps the player-selected attack objective/facing visually and tactically dominant.

## 3. Future cavalry order: SPEJD HER
`SPEJD HER` is approved as a future mounted-cavalry reconnaissance order, but is not exposed as a live button until the true FOG/LOS system owns enemy visibility.

Expected sequence:

`SPEJD HER → SEEK/RECON → CONTACT → SCREEN → REPORT / LAST-KNOWN CONTACT`

Rules:
- The clicked point/circle is a reconnaissance area, not an attack objective.
- CAV travels toward the area using normal terrain/enemy avoidance.
- On contact, CAV maintains observation at a safe weapon-aware stand-off distance.
- CAV gives ground if enemy infantry closes aggressively.
- Sightings are reported to `CurrentCommandParent` and propagate upward through later command-delay/courier simulation.
- Reports should include unit type, approximate strength, observed position, observation time and confidence/quality.
- Lost LOS creates a last-known contact; information does not remain perfectly current.
- `SPEJD HER` does not itself authorize CHARGE.
- The order is hidden/disabled when real FOG/LOS is not active.
