# PROJECT 1864 — v00.00.09f29j Pre-contact Line Deployment

## Design rule
Infantry may use march column while approaching contact, but must not knowingly cross into hostile LONG-range fire while still in column.

For an approaching company:
- determine the nearest credible enemy formation,
- use that enemy formation's `MaximumRange` as the hostile LONG-range boundary,
- begin/force deployment to Line approximately **10 m outside** that boundary,
- preserve movement authority: the formation policy may change Column → Line but must not rewrite the Major/Captain movement destination,
- once deployed, require an additional safety margin before returning to Column so an enemy hovering around the threshold cannot cause Line/Column oscillation,
- an active Infantry Square always has stronger formation authority and must not be collapsed by this policy.

## Higher-command movement
A Major may temporarily disable the local `OfficerAIController` while physically moving a company to its assigned slot. This no longer blocks safety-critical pre-contact deployment. The Major continues to own movement; the approach policy only changes the formation to Line when the company reaches enemy LONG range + deployment buffer.

## QA
1. Order a company/battalion to attack while companies are marching in Column.
2. Observe the nearest enemy's LONG-range ring.
3. The company must start changing to Line before entering that ring, not after receiving the first volley.
4. The company must continue toward the assigned objective; no new movement route may be created by the formation policy.
5. If distance fluctuates around the LONG boundary, the company must not repeatedly flip Line ↔ Column.
6. SQUARE must remain SQUARE and must not be overwritten by pre-contact deployment.
