# v00.00.09f23 — Manual authority + collision-safe formation slots

Status: TEST design/runtime supplement.

## 1. Manual company authority is immediate

A direct player order to one or more companies is authoritative for those companies.

Runtime rule:

1. Selected company receives direct player order.
2. Its Officer AI is switched OFF **before** PlayerCommander consumes the input.
3. Its live Major mission is removed.
4. Its HQ mission visual is removed.
5. The company is marked manually detached.
6. Only the new player route/order remains authoritative.

The old f18 execution order ran the manual-authority layer after normal PlayerCommander input processing. f23 moves authority release before the normal order pipeline.

The Major may reclaim the company only when:

- the player gives the Major a new battalion order, or
- the player manually switches the company AI ON again.

Simply having Major AI ON must never silently steal back an AI-OFF company.

## 2. Formation slots are solved as a set

Major and manual multi-company Line placement now share the same slot-legality rules.

A slot is illegal when the complete company footprint would:

- overlap Farmhouse/Barn hard blockers,
- end in open river water,
- leave battlefield bounds, or
- overlap an already reserved company slot.

Approximate company centre spacing remains 60 m, with a minimum reservation separation of 55 m.

## 3. Obstacle correction preserves the requested line

If a requested slot is blocked, the resolver searches along the requested formation line first.

Search priority:

1. requested slot,
2. small left/right corrections along the same line,
3. progressively larger left/right corrections,
4. only as a last resort, a shallow fore/aft stagger while continuing to search laterally.

This prevents two blocked companies from both being projected onto the same building edge.

The player-drawn line / Major objective remains the tactical reference. Obstacle avoidance should distort the line only as much as necessary.

## 4. Unique slot reservation

Once a legal slot is accepted, it is reserved immediately. Later companies may not receive the same or an overlapping footprint.

This specifically fixes:

- two companies ending on top of each other after FORSVAR HER near buildings,
- manually drawn group Lines where one company stood on a building and another fell far off the line,
- independent endpoint correction collapsing several companies onto one legal edge.

## 5. Nearest-company assignment

After the final legal slot set is generated, the companies are assigned to those slots by minimum total squared march distance across all available permutations (2–4 companies).

This avoids unnecessary route crossing and gives each company the nearest sensible free place rather than binding slots to internal list order.

## 6. Manual and Major planners use the same concept

The shared runtime utility is:

`PrototypeFormationSlotSafety09F23`

Used by:

- manual multi-company Line placement,
- Major battalion formation deconfliction.

The Major-specific final authority layer is:

`PrototypeMajorSlotDeconfliction09F23`

It runs after the f22 objective-centred planner and preserves manual AI-OFF authority.

## 7. Deferred: true sub-formation obstacle threading

f23 does **not** yet visually split one company into independent left/right sub-columns around a building and reform it afterward.

That remains a later path-threading feature. f23 instead guarantees that the company's final formation footprint is legal and that multi-company final positions do not overlap.

3. Every final company footprint must be clear of the building.
4. The front should remain on/near the requested line and only extend sideways as needed.
5. Draw a manual multi-company Line through/near the same building.
6. Manual slots must follow the same legality/reservation rule.
7. Units should be assigned to the nearest final slots without unnecessary crossing.
8. While a Major mission is active, manually select one or more companies and give a direct order.
