# v00.00.09f30x — Early Deploy, Company Spacing, CAV Bridge Approach & Startup Enemy Cones

## 1. Infantry deploy before enemy fire range
Previous march-column logic stayed in Column until the nearest enemy was already at/inside MaximumRange.

F30X changes tactical deployment to:

`DeployDistance = max(own MaximumRange, nearest enemy MaximumRange) + 35 m`

With the current 100 m TEST MaximumRange, a company starts physical Line reform at about 135 m.

The purpose is not to extend firing range. It gives the three-rank formation enough space/time to reform before entering the enemy's actual fire envelope.

### Authority
- parent mission owns destination;
- MarchColumn owns normal Line/Column movement policy;
- F27 no longer forces Line on each periodic destination reassert.

## 2. Company separation
Current full-strength 190-man three-rank frontage is roughly 48 m.

F30X changes:
- company nominal spacing: 60 m -> 72 m;
- minimum reserved centre separation: 55 m -> 68 m.

This adds lateral clearance for oblique formations and reduces visual/physical overlap.

## 3. Cavalry bridge approach
Previously an opposite-bank destination immediately set `bridgePhase=NearBank`. Since bridge-phase visuals are two-abreast, cavalry could become a long two-abreast column hundreds of metres from the bridge.

F30X separates:
- `bridgeRoutePlanned=true`: normal formation while travelling toward bridge;
- actual narrow bridge transaction: activates at about 36 m from near-bank approach;
- NearBank/FarBank/ExitBank: two-abreast;
- after exit clearance: restore the formation held immediately before narrow mode.

Normal cavalry movement remains:
- Line = 4 ranks;
- normal Column = 4 abreast;
- bridge/defile = 2 abreast only near/on the bridge.

## 4. Enemy cone startup
The TEST overlay no longer assumes CloseRangeFan/MediumRangeFan/LongRangeFan already exist.

If a living Prussian Regiment exists but a fan child is missing, F30X creates the missing GameObject + LineRenderer immediately in the late QA pass.

This ensures enemy cones become visible from initial battle setup rather than after selection or another visual state creates the renderer.
