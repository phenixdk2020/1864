# v00.00.09f20 – Selection handoff, commander intent and doctrine response

Status: **ACTIVE PROTOTYPE RULESET**

This supplement refines the unified officer-AI model from 09f18/09f19 after QA showed two authority problems: Major selection could prevent normal company selection, and Major doctrine/autonomous thinking could override or appear to ignore a player-issued battalion mission.

## 1. Selection authority

Major and company selection are mutually exclusive UI contexts.

- Selecting the Major opens the Major/HQ panel and clears company selection.
- Clicking a Danish company after the Major is selected transfers selection authority to the company immediately.
- Box-selecting one or more Danish companies also transfers selection authority away from the Major.
- The Major panel closes and the normal company bottom bar returns.
- Merely selecting a company is **not** a manual tactical order and therefore does **not** switch its AI OFF.

The existing authority rule remains:

> Company AI switches OFF only when the player gives that company a direct manual order.

## 2. Player Major order = commander intent

A player-issued Major mission is an operational/tactical intent, not a suggestion that the Major may silently replace six seconds later.

Examples:

- `FORSVAR HER`
- `ANGRIB HER`
- `TILBAGETRÆK HERTIL`
- `RYK FREM HERTIL`
- `HOLD POSITION`
- `SAML HER`

With Major AI ON, the Major may interpret **how** to execute the mission:

- company placement;
- facing;
- whether a reserve is useful;
- whether a reserve should flank;
- local reactions inside the mission;
- later officer personality/disobedience rules.

But in the current prototype he may not silently replace the player's explicit mission with a different high-level mission.

This is the intended hierarchy:

**Player/Higher HQ intent → Major interpretation → company tasks.**

## 3. Doctrine is not a replacement order

`DEF / BAL / OFF` is doctrine/posture, not a new target location.

If the player has already ordered `FORSVAR HER` at point A:

- switching to OFF does not cancel point A;
- switching to DEF does not invent a new defensive point;
- the Major re-plans the same mission around point A using the new doctrine.

This distinction is important for later officer stats: an aggressive Major may execute a defensive mission more actively, but the mission itself remains the commander's intent unless future disobedience logic explicitly decides otherwise.

## 4. Doctrine with no explicit player mission

If Major AI is ON and there is no active explicit player mission:

- **DEF** immediately establishes a defensive posture around the battalion's current position;
- **BAL** returns control to normal autonomous assessment;
- **OFF** returns control to normal autonomous assessment with offensive doctrine.

This prevents a Major from visibly remaining on an old offensive plan after the player changes him to DEF.

## 5. Interaction with company authority

The 09f18 rules remain unchanged:

- new Major order → subordinate companies AI ON and old manual routes cleared;
- direct manual company order → that company AI OFF and detached from the current Major mission;
- company AI ON again → may rejoin its stored Major assignment;
- selecting the company alone does not detach it.

## 6. Interaction with reserve/flank AI

09f19 reserve/flank logic remains active while the Major executes a player mission.

Therefore `FORSVAR HER` can still produce, when justified:

- four companies in line;
- three companies in line + one reserve;
- three companies in line + one reserve used for a local flank/counter-flank.

The Major is free to decide subordinate tactics, but not free to abandon the ordered defensive objective in the current prototype.

## 7. Future officer disobedience

Later officer personality/stat systems may intentionally allow deviation from commander intent.

That future behavior must be explicit and explainable through stats such as:

- Discipline / Obedience;
- Initiative;
- Aggressiveness / Caution;
- Tactical Skill;
- Composure;
- Experience.

Until that system is implemented, mission replacement caused by ordinary autonomous `ThinkMajor` logic is considered a bug, not personality.

## 8. QA telemetry

Relevant console prefixes:

- `SELECTION-09F20` – company selection successfully took authority from Major;
- `MAJOR-INTENT-09F20` – explicit Major intent captured or doctrine caused re-planning;
- `AI-AUTHORITY-09F18` – company/Major AI authority transitions;
- `HQ-RESERVE-09F19` – discretionary reserve/flank decision.
