# v00.00.09f9 – Bridge-aware attack routing

Status: **IMPLEMENTED / TEST**

## Problem

An explicit `ANGRIB` mission could stop at the river or repeatedly step into open water. The cause was architectural: the officer/attack logic could decide that the enemy was already inside preferred engagement range and call `Regiment.OrderAttack(target)`. That direct target tracking continuously wrote the enemy position as movement destination. The river constraint ran later in the frame, so it could restore the last safe position but could not prevent the direct movement step that had already happened.

The repeated Console telemetry `OpenWaterPrevented=True|RestoredLastSafe=True` was therefore a symptom of competing movement writers, not a missing bridge collider.

## v00.00.09f9 routing rule

Bridge routing has higher priority than Euclidean attack range.

Before an officer is allowed to close/engage a target, `OfficerAIController.EngageEnemy()` asks `PrototypeRiverBridgeOnly09F3.TryGetAttackSteering()` whether open water lies between the company and the designated target.

If a bridge route is required:

1. the attack target remains the strategic/final intent;
2. the company is forced into `Column`;
3. movement is temporarily redirected through bridge staging points;
4. `OrderAttack()` is not issued while the river still separates attacker and target;
5. after the complete company has cleared the far staging point, normal attack-range logic resumes;
6. the company may then redeploy to `Line`, aim and fire according to the current F9 fire policy.

This applies to both player-designated `ANGRIB` missions executed through Officer AI and normal offensive AI decisions using the same `EngageEnemy()` path.

## Bridge corridor / visual behaviour

The crossing is no longer a single sharp turn at the bridge edge.

The bridge route now contains four functional positions along the bridge axis:

- **near staging point:** approximately 32 m from bridge centre on the current bank;
- **near entry point:** approximately 13 m from bridge centre;
- **far entry point:** approximately 13 m beyond bridge centre;
- **far staging point:** approximately 32 m beyond bridge centre.

Sequence:

`STAGE_BRIDGE -> APPROACH_BRIDGE -> CROSS_BRIDGE -> EXIT_BRIDGE -> DIRECT`

The unit changes to marching column as soon as the bridge route is identified. It therefore has dry ground and distance available to reform before entering the narrow crossing. From the near staging point to the far staging point, the route lies on the bridge axis, reducing the previous visual problem where the long formation pivoted sideways across the water.

The company stays in Column until its centre reaches the far staging point. Only after that point is the normal Line-deployment policy allowed to take over.

## Navigation ownership

The current isolation baseline is retained:

- trees/buildings/fences: pass-through;
- river/open water: hard movement barrier;
- fixed bridge at `z=22`: only legal river crossing;
- old V1/V3/V4/09A/recovery obstacle navigation remains disabled.

The bridge router remains a targeted river constraint, not a return to full obstacle pathfinding.

## Safety / telemetry

The open-water safety restoration remains as a last-resort guard. In normal 09f9 bridge routing it should no longer trigger repeatedly.

If another movement system writes an illegal water path, the warning is rate-limited to about once per second per unit:

`RIVER-09F9|...|OpenWaterPrevented=True|RestoredLastSafe=True|UnexpectedWriter=True`

Normal crossing telemetry includes:

- `BridgeRoute=True`
- `Phase=STAGE_BRIDGE`
- `Phase=APPROACH_BRIDGE`
- `Phase=CROSS_BRIDGE`
- `Phase=EXIT_BRIDGE`
- `CrossingComplete=True`

## Acceptance test

1. Select the Danish company and issue `ANGRIB` against the Prussian company across the river.
2. The unit must not walk directly toward the river bank and stop there.
3. It must change to Column and route to the near staging point.
4. It must align with the bridge before reaching the water.
5. It must cross only on the bridge.
6. The long column/selection footprint should remain visually aligned with the bridge axis.
7. It must remain in Column until the far staging point is reached.
8. After clearing the crossing, the original attack mission resumes and the company may deploy into Line at engagement range.
9. Repeated `OpenWaterPrevented=True` warnings should not occur during a normal crossing.
10. 09f8/09f9 combat QA, 35/70/100 m range tuning, formation-ready fire cone, 16.8 deg/s stationary facing, volley animation, smoke and Forced March remain retained.
