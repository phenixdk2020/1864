# v00.00.09f12 – Route authority fix

Status: IMPLEMENTED FOR TEST

## Problem observed in 09f11

Two route-ownership bugs remained after the 09f11 terrain pass.

1. **Direct ATTACK bypassed pathfinding.** `Regiment` continuously tracks its `forcedTarget` and rewrites `destination` to the enemy position before movement. That could overwrite bridge/building steering and make the unit attempt the straight line again.
2. **Repeated final destinations reset building detours.** Player/AI command layers can repeat the same final destination every frame. The 09f11 static router treated any destination different from its current steering waypoint as a new order, cleared the queue, and rebuilt the same first detour.

## 09f12 route ownership

A new `PrototypeAttackRouteAuthority09F12` runs before normal Regiment movement.

For an ATTACK mission:

1. the enemy remains the strategic target;
2. if river crossing is required, river/bridge routing owns physical steering;
3. otherwise, if Farmhouse/Barn blocks the direct approach, static-building routing owns physical steering;
4. while a terrain router owns the route, direct `forcedTarget` tracking is temporarily suspended so `Regiment.Update()` cannot overwrite the legal steering point;
5. as soon as the current position again has a clear legal approach to the target, the original forced target is restored and normal attack-range/fire logic resumes.

This means terrain routing changes *how the unit reaches the enemy*, not *which enemy was ordered attacked*.

## Building waypoint correction

`PrototypeStaticObstacleRouting09F11` now distinguishes three destination cases:

- current router waypoint;
- the already-known final goal;
- a genuinely new external goal.

Repeated writes of the **same final goal** no longer clear the current waypoint queue. A two-corner detour can therefore progress from corner 1 to corner 2 and then continue to the final destination.

### Overlapping hard blockers

The current Farmhouse and Barn are close enough that their safety clearances overlap for a full Line formation. 09f12 therefore merges overlapping expanded blocker rectangles into one temporary obstacle cluster when computing the detour. The company routes around the outside of the compound instead of trying to squeeze between house and barn.

## Priority

Current movement priority is:

1. rout/panic safety;
2. river/bridge legality;
3. approved hard-building avoidance (`Farmhouse`, `Barn`);
4. explicit ATTACK/MOVE mission goal;
5. normal Line/Column policy;
6. fire/engagement logic.

The older V1/V3/V4 obstacle-navigation stack remains disabled.

## Retained terrain rules

- River: hard blocker, bridge only.
- Farmhouse: hard blocker + automatic detour.
- Barn: hard blocker + automatic detour.
- Trees: pass-through for now.
- Fence posts: pass-through for now.
- Roads: passable.
- Bridge deck: passable legal crossing.

## Retained map scale

- Battlefield: 1440 x 960 m.
- 1 Unity unit = 1 metre.
- River remains extended over the enlarged battlefield.
- 09f11 camera/map expansion remains unchanged.
