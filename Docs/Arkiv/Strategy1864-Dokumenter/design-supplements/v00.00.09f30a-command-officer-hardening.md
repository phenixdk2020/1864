# PROJECT 1864 — v00.00.09f30a

## Command, Square and Officer AI hardening

Status: **TEST**.

F30A is a regression/hardening pass on top of the first F30 cavalry core. It does not replace the mounted prototype. Its purpose is to make infantry command-state, Square transitions, officer facing, attack-role allocation, pre-contact deployment, friendly frontage behaviour and cavalry command UX consistent enough for proper combined-arms QA.

## 1. OOB selection persistence

An entity selected through OOB is the same command selection as an entity selected in the 3D battlefield.

- single-click in OOB selects without moving the camera;
- HUD button presses must not clear the selection;
- completing a pending point-order must not clear the selection;
- company, Major and Oberstløjtnant selections are all covered;
- explicit later user selection remains authoritative;
- camera position is unchanged by this persistence layer.

## 2. Persistent regimental order state

Regimental order buttons use blue as the pending/active mission state.

- click an order: it becomes blue immediately;
- after the point is committed, the same order remains blue while subordinate missions execute it;
- the state is removed when no living subordinate unit remains under that order or a different order replaces it;
- this is UI state only and does not create a second command authority.

## 3. Square presentation and smoke hardening

The old Line/mission footprint must not remain visible while a Square is forming or active.

Square sector/range lines are terrain-conformed with extra height clearance so short segments do not disappear into small terrain undulations.

A formation transition is never a volley. Square enter/exit therefore synchronises legacy timer-based visual detectors so:

- LINE -> SQUARE does not emit fake smoke;
- SQUARE -> LINE does not emit fake smoke;
- only a recorded real volley can create black-powder smoke or a firing pose.

## 4. FORSVAR HER: objective and facing

The player's clicked objective is authoritative.

If an explicit facing arrow is dragged:

- the defensive line is built around the clicked objective;
- frontage is perpendicular to that facing;
- all company mission facings use the explicit facing;
- river/formation safety may repair an illegal individual slot but may not move the entire mission away from the objective.

If no arrow is dragged, AUTO facing is resolved as:

1. nearest relevant enemy from the objective;
2. otherwise the current average front direction of the selected battalion/regiment;
3. never `orderPoint - currentCenter` merely because that vector happens to point backwards.

## 5. ATTACK HERE: strongest companies in assault roles

The Major uses current combat strength and experience when assigning companies to attack roles.

QA score:

`AssaultPower = CurrentStrength × (0.60 + 0.40 × Experience/100)`

Role priority:

- strongest available companies -> assault/front roles;
- next strongest -> flank/support role when present;
- weakest suitable company -> pure reserve role.

This score does **not** override terrain, bridge, route, charge or other safety rules.

## 6. Early Column -> Line deployment

A company must be physically ready in Line **before** it enters the enemy's Long/MaximumRange.

F29J began deployment only 10 m outside hostile MaximumRange. Field QA showed that the visual three-rank reform could still be underway when hostile fire range was crossed.

F30A starts the change approximately:

`hostile MaximumRange + 35 m`

This is a QA buffer, chosen to give roughly ten seconds of formation movement at normal infantry march speeds. The exact final historical/gameplay value can later be data-driven by formation-change time, unit experience and movement speed.

Bridge crossing remains the explicit exception: bridge routing can force Column even inside hostile Long range.

## 7. Friendly fire-lane Side Step

F29P Side Step detected only close physical overlap (~13 m). That is insufficient because two full company formations can be tens of metres apart and still obstruct the same firing lane.

F30A adds fire-lane deconfliction:

- a firing Line company with a live target defines a direct friendly-fire lane between formation and target;
- a friendly company occupying that lane is considered an obstruction even without physical overlap;
- the tactical correction is a left/right Side Step, not a 90-degree turn or Column manoeuvre;
- facing and Line are preserved;
- water/blocked endpoints are rejected;
- Square, bridge routing, charge, melee, under-fire emergency reaction and immediate close contact retain higher priority;
- direct manual forced-target authority is not silently displaced by autonomous Side Step.

When deciding which company moves:

- if the front company is not productively engaging the enemy, the blocking formation steps out;
- if the front company is already effectively firing at the same target, that established frontage is preserved and the rear company steps left/right to obtain its own clear lane.

The local lane correction is temporary authority. The parent mission remains conceptually active and may resume once the lane is cleared.

## 8. AI-officer withdrawal capability — committed rule

A Kaptajn/Major must eventually be able to choose a controlled fighting withdrawal without explicit player input.

The decision may use:

- recent and total casualties;
- current men vs. local enemy strength;
- morale/cohesion;
- ammunition;
- flank/rear threat;
- availability of reserve/support;
- terrain and route safety;
- officer Tactical Skill, Initiative, Experience, Composure, Discipline and Aggressiveness.

Withdrawal is not automatic panic. A disciplined/experienced officer may remain longer under pressure; another may withdraw earlier to preserve the company. The existing fighting-withdrawal movement system remains the execution mechanism when this decision layer is implemented.

## 9. Cavalry uses the normal tactical unit flow

The temporary F30 `CAVALRY TEST [F30]` / F10 control panel is removed from normal QA. Gardehusar and Dragon are tested as battlefield units, not from a parallel debug command system.

F30A interaction baseline:

- click Gardehusar/Dragon in the 3D battlefield -> select the cavalry unit;
- cavalry also appears directly beneath the normal OOB as company-scale (`I`) cavalry rows;
- OOB single-click selects only and does **not** move the camera;
- OOB double-click selects and places the camera behind the cavalry unit, matching infantry/HQ navigation;
- a selected cavalry unit uses the same 90 px bottom-command area and visual language as the infantry command HUD;
- right-click terrain -> MOVE;
- right-click an enemy infantry company while mounted -> CHARGE;
- `CHARGE`, `STOP / HOLD`, `LINJE`, `KOLONNE` are available in the bottom HUD;
- Dragon additionally has `SID AF / STIG OP`;
- infantry/HQ and cavalry selection are mutually exclusive so two command HUDs cannot remain active at once.

This is **command-flow integration**, not a claim that cavalry already has the complete infantry feature set. Cavalry Officer AI, mounted firearms and dismounted Dragon fire remain subsequent F30 work.
