# PROJECT 1864 — v00.00.09f29m Square Active-Renderer Fix

## Problem found during F29L QA
The Square tactical state activated, but the displayed soldiers remained in Line/Column and the normal rectangular selection footprint remained visible.

The concrete runtime cause was renderer authority:

- `PrototypeInfantrySquare09F29` and the first F29L visual Square layer reflected `PrototypeBattleVisuals09F5.states`.
- `PrototypeBattleVisuals09F7` is the current visible company renderer.
- F7 explicitly disables F5 at runtime and keeps its own independent `states` / `LocalPositions` collection.
- The Square system therefore moved soldier positions belonging to an inactive renderer while F7 continued drawing the visible soldiers in Line/Column.
- `PrototypeUnitFootprint09F5` also kept redrawing the normal rectangular Line/Column selection footprint and collider in LateUpdate.

## F29M authority fix
`PrototypeSquareRuntimeHotfix09F29M` is the final visual authority while a company is in Square.

It:
- reads the active `PrototypeBattleVisuals09F7.states` dictionary;
- writes the visible F7 `LocalPositions` after F7 performs its ordinary Line/Column update;
- interpolates the visible men from their current positions into the hollow two-rank Square over the existing Square formation timer;
- sizes the Square from `CurrentStrength` so losses reduce its footprint;
- hides `SelectionFootprint09F5` while Square is active;
- reapplies the Square collider after the normal footprint system runs;
- disables the obsolete F29L Square visual writer that targeted F5.

The tactical Square state itself remains owned by `PrototypeInfantrySquare09F29`: formation timer, HOLD FIRE while forming, Square-ready state, fire reduction and anti-cavalry modifiers are unchanged.

## Expected presentation
When SQUARE is pressed:

1. SQUARE becomes the active formation state.
2. The long yellow Line/Column selection rectangle disappears immediately.
3. `DANNER KARRÉ` remains visible during formation.
4. Individual displayed men visibly leave Line/Column and move toward all four sides of a hollow Square.
5. When the existing timer expires, the status becomes `KARRÉ KLAR`.
6. A company with fewer surviving men forms a visibly smaller Square.
7. When LINJE or KOLONNE is selected, Square state ends and the ordinary formation footprint may reappear.
