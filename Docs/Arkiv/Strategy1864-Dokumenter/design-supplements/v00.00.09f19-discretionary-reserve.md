# v00.00.09f19 – Discretionary reserve and flank judgement

Status: **ACTIVE PROTOTYPE RULESET**

This supplement refines the reserve logic introduced in v00.00.09f18.

## Core rule

A reserve is a tactical judgement by the Major, not a fixed template and not a direct function of how many enemy units are known.

Therefore:

- 4 friendly companies versus 1 known enemy company can still produce **3 FRONT + 1 RESERVE**;
- the Major may instead use all four in the front if battalion condition is poor or the threat is too strong;
- creating a reserve does **not** automatically imply a flank manoeuvre;
- a flank is a second, stricter decision based on condition, distance, route length and legal terrain.

## Current 4v1 prototype thresholds

A reserve may be created for `ANGRIB HER` or `FORSVAR HER` when all four companies are active and:

- average battalion strength is at least 68%;
- average cohesion is at least 54;
- total visible friendly strength is at least 2.65 times the single known enemy's visible strength.

These are QA values, not final historical doctrine values.

The reserve candidate is the freshest / most cohesive company.

The other three companies are re-centred side by side with the existing 60 m company centre spacing.

## Flank judgement

The reserve may be sent to a flank only when the tactical situation is stronger than the reserve gate itself.

Current prototype requirements include:

- average battalion strength at least 76%;
- average cohesion at least 62;
- reserve company strength at least 75%;
- enemy within a useful tactical distance;
- route to the proposed flank not excessively long;
- legal formation endpoint according to the existing hard-obstacle / river checks.

`ANGRIB HER` allows a wider flank opportunity than `FORSVAR HER`.

A defensive flank represents a local counter-flank / counterattack option rather than an automatic offensive sweep.

## One-enemy flank side

With only one known enemy unit there is no meaningful enemy-count asymmetry between left and right.

For that case the Major compares the two legal flank options and uses the side requiring the shorter reserve movement, provided the flank gate is otherwise satisfied.

Later this should also include:

- terrain quality;
- roads and bridges;
- woods / concealment;
- artillery threat;
- enemy facing;
- known reserves;
- officer tactical skill, initiative, aggression and caution;
- delayed / incomplete battlefield intelligence.

## UI / telemetry

The Major panel should make the decision visible through `LastDecisionText`, for example:

- `Reserve: 4. Kmp | 4 mod 1 | styrkeoverlegenhed x3.8 | holdes bag front`
- `Reserve: 2. Kmp | 4 mod 1 | styrkeoverlegenhed x3.5 | FLANKE vurderet fordelagtig`

Console telemetry uses:

`HQ-RESERVE-09F19`

and includes reserve company, strength superiority, average condition and flank decision.

## Future rule

The final system must not be deterministic from raw unit count alone.

Two Majors in the same tactical situation may reach different decisions because of officer stats, personality, doctrine, information quality and previous battlefield experience.
