# v00.00.09f30v — Single Final-Slot Arrival Authority

## Problem
QA showed companies visually stopping before their blue destination footprints while the parent `FORSVAR HER` order returned red.

The destination footprint itself was not the mismatch: both the footprint and company mission use the same `mission.Goal`.

The actual problem was layered completion logic:
- F27 core mission arrival: 4.5 m;
- F29E precise-arrival correction: 0.85 m;
- F29L DefendHere latch: 1.35 m.

That allowed different systems to disagree about when movement was finished.

## F30V authority
One final-slot rule is used for company parent missions:

**FinalSlotArrival = 0.50 m**

This is deliberately close to `Regiment.UpdateMovement()`, which clears its own destination at ~0.45 m.

### F27
Mission `Arrived=true` only when company root is within 0.50 m of `mission.Goal`.

### F29E
Precise-arrival compatibility uses the same 0.50 m threshold. Its old broad 5 m correction window is reduced to 0.75 m for stale/hot-reload compatibility only.

### F29L
DefendHere latch uses 0.50 m and releases if the company later drifts beyond 1.25 m.

## HUD execution rule
A parent order remains active/blue if any company:
- is more than 0.50 m from its mission goal;
- still has an active Regiment movement destination;
- has `Arrived=false`.

Therefore a stale `Arrived=true` flag cannot make Division/Brigade/Regiment/Major order status red while a company is still physically short of its slot.

## Tactical interruptions
Under-fire reaction may temporarily stop a moving unit, but does not complete the parent mission. After the interruption ends, F27 can reassert the same mission goal.

## QA
1. Issue Division `FORSVAR HER` to a clearly displaced point.
2. Compare each company root/formation centre to its blue footprint.
3. Button remains blue until every company is physically at its slot.
4. No company should stop permanently 1–5 m short due to an arrival latch.
5. Re-issue the order before completion; new goals replace the old ones cleanly.
6. Test temporary under-fire pause; mission remains active and resumes afterwards.
