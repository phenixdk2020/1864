# v00.00.09f30l — Cavalry Visual Fidelity + Gait Polish

## Scope
F30L is intentionally a visual/animation pass. F30K remains the gameplay-authority baseline for cavalry movement, formation, bridge routing, AI, higher-HQ control and Dragon dismounted combat.

## 1:1 rule
Gardehusar remain 120/120 and Dragon 140/140 at tactical scale. F30L may reduce **detail rendering** at distance, but it never changes simulated strength or tactical figure count.

## Horse fidelity
The existing F30E horse transforms are reshaped rather than replaced with another parallel model system. The pass improves proportions of:
- body/chest;
- neck;
- head/muzzle;
- legs/hooves;
- mane/tail.

Six deterministic coat tones are distributed across each squadron. Sparse blaze/sock markings create additional variation.

## Rider identity
Existing F30E/H identity remains authoritative:
- Gardehusar: light-blue coat, silver/white braid, red pelisse accents, dark busby/plume and sabre.
- Dragon: dark-blue coat, red facings, crested helmet, carbine and sabre.

F30L adds close-detail equipment:
- Gardehusar sabretache/trim;
- Dragon cartridge box/badge;
- shared breast collar, girth and stirrups.

## Animation
Mounted gait now articulates the four horse legs and hooves in diagonal pairs. Movement also drives head nod, tail swing and rider lean/bounce.

Normal MOVE uses a restrained cadence. CHARGE uses:
- higher cadence;
- greater leg swing;
- stronger head motion;
- stronger rider forward lean.

HOLD restores the articulated parts toward the upgraded F30L base pose.

## Performance / LOD
The added F30L close-detail roots are disabled above approximately 210m camera altitude. This is visual LOD only; no soldier/horse is removed from the simulation or tactical 1:1 count.

## QA status
F30L remains TEST until Unity 6000.6.0f1 runtime QA confirms visual quality and acceptable performance with both full squadrons present.
