# PROJECT 1864 — v00.00.09f29l Battlefield Hardening

## Scope
F29L bundles four runtime problems found during F29K QA into one test revision:

1. Major/Oberstløjtnant HQ follow distance and follow authority.
2. River water continuity / terrain clipping.
3. Full visible infantry Square reform.
4. Defensive destination oscillation.

The revision is deliberately additive and keeps F29K charge targeting, F29J pre-contact deployment, F29I tactical visibility and F29G command/HUD behaviour intact.

## HQ follow
AI ON/OFF controls autonomous decision-making, not whether an HQ physically follows an already-issued order.

### Major
- Preferred position: about **105 m behind** the battalion centre relative to the mission/front direction.
- Reposition when the desired follow point has shifted by about **55 m**.
- A manual HQ move remains authoritative until a new mission supersedes it.

### Oberstløjtnant
- Preferred position: about **210 m behind** the midpoint of the two Majors.
- Reposition when the desired follow point has shifted by about **90 m**.
- The Oberstløjtnant follows player-issued/higher-command missions even with regimental AI OFF.
- Manual HQ relocation remains authoritative until the next mission.

For withdrawal, HQs stay on the safe rear side in the direction of withdrawal rather than being placed toward the enemy.

## River rendering
The F29G overlay was only 0.075 m above an analytically sampled stream bed. The enlarged battlefield terrain mesh uses much coarser cells, so the actually rendered ground can sit above that analytic sample and visually cover the water.

F29L therefore:
- raycasts the actual `Battlefield Ground` mesh,
- builds one continuous river ribbon from map edge to map edge,
- uses 1.25 m longitudinal sampling,
- offsets width perpendicular to the curved stream tangent,
- uses a 7.5 m visible water width,
- lifts the water surface 0.24 m above the rendered stream bed,
- disables older visible Stream segments / F29G ribbon while leaving navigation/colliders unchanged,
- continues underneath the bridge.

## Visual Square reform
Square remains the F29 combat state, but F29L owns the final displayed-soldier positions while Square is active.

Behaviour:
- men visibly leave Line/Column and move toward a hollow square;
- the square uses two perimeter ranks;
- the movement is synchronized to the existing Square formation time rather than snapping immediately;
- the target square footprint scales with `CurrentStrength`;
- losses therefore produce a smaller square;
- the physical collider scales with the current square footprint;
- the existing `DANNER KARRÉ` / `KARRÉ KLAR` state and anti-cavalry/fire rules remain authoritative.

This is a visual-position authority only. It does not create a second movement system for the regiment root.

## Defensive arrival hysteresis
A defensive company must not repeatedly walk into and out of its blue destination footprint.

F29L uses two thresholds:
- **1.35 m**: latch the company as arrived;
- **3.20 m**: release the latch only after meaningful drift.

While latched:
- ordinary destination movement is held;
- F29E's old 0.85 m micro-correction is suppressed;
- the destination remains visually completed;
- under-fire reaction retains tactical authority and may temporarily move the company;
- after local reaction ends, a materially displaced company may be released and return to its defensive slot.

## QA

2. Give Oberstløjtnant `ANGRIB HER` with AI OFF: both Majors and Oberstløjtnant must continue following the formation instead of remaining far behind.
3. Verify Majors remain roughly 105 m behind their battalion and Oberstløjtnant roughly 210 m behind the Major midpoint, subject to terrain/navigation safety.
4. Manually move a HQ and verify the explicit player move is not immediately overwritten; issue a new mission and verify normal follow resumes.
5. Inspect the full river and bridge: water must be continuous with no dry visual gaps and must continue beneath the bridge.
6. Select a company and order SQUARE: displayed men must visibly move from their current formation into a hollow two-rank square over the formation timer.
7. Repeat after losses: the resulting Square must visibly be smaller.
8. Give `FORSVAR HER`: after a company reaches its blue destination it must settle and stop shuttling back/forth around the slot.
9. Regression: F29K CHARGE remains green while armed, confirms with right-click, and holds fire during the charge.
10. Regression: F29J Column -> Line before enemy LONG range remains active.
