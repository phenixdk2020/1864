# v00.00.09f30i — Cavalry authority, single HUD, order visuals & animation

## Authority
Cavalry Officer AI now defaults to OFF/MANUEL. No cavalry unit may autonomously move from spawn until the player explicitly enables AI or gives a manual order.

The F30H AI toggle bug came from treating every bottom-HUD click as a manual override in Update before OnGUI handled the AI button. F30I limits that automatic override to direct world right-click orders. HUD commands explicitly choose when to disable AI.

## Single HUD owner
PrototypeCavalryManager09F30 is the sole cavalry HUD renderer. The F30C cavalry control strip and F2 legacy bottom bar retain nonvisual helper/gate logic only.

## Selection and order readability
Selected cavalry gets a yellow formation-sized rectangle rather than the old small cylinder. The footprint smoothly follows four-rank Line, four-abreast Column and two-abreast bridge states.

A selected unit with an active destination shows:
- pale order path;
- current bridge steering point when relevant;
- yellow destination ghost box using the planned final footprint.

## Initial 1:1 formation
After F30E expands the old proxy list to 120/140 figures, F30I snaps the completed list once into the correct four-rank initial slots. Runtime formation changes remain animated.

## Mount/dismount and riding
Dragon SID AF/STIG OP uses a procedural transition of approximately 2.25 seconds. Mounted movement has a procedural gait; charge uses a stronger/faster cycle. This is a temporary low-poly animation layer until dedicated rigged horse/rider assets are introduced.

## OOB
OOB width is increased to 535 px. Fixed right columns and scroll behavior remain unchanged.
