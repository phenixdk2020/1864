# B-281 – Manual attack authority isolation

Status: AKTIV / 09f21

A direct player attack on one company must override the current Major mission for that company and must remain isolated from Major/group attack frontage logic until the player returns the company to AI control or a new Major order explicitly reclaims it.

Acceptance:
- single manual attack cannot be converted into a shared Major attack lane;
- AI ON Major companies cannot be mixed into manual attack-lane grouping;
- 2+ direct player-controlled companies may still receive side-by-side manual attack lanes;
- terrain legality (river/bridge/buildings) retains priority;
- a new Major order may reclaim the company and switch AI ON according to the unified authority rule.
