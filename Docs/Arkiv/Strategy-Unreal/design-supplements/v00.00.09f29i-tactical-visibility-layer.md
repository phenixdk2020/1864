# PROJECT 1864 — v00.00.09f29i Tactical Visibility Layer

## Purpose
F29I extends the existing F29D semantic/NATO zoom with an immediate tactical-readability layer for normal battle play. The goal is to make command hierarchy, current intent, facing and active combat readable without forcing the player to zoom all the way out to Operational/Strategic view.

## Design rules

### 1. HQs must remain discoverable
- Below the F29D NATO threshold, Major and Oberstløjtnant HQs receive fixed-size screen-space labels and a beacon down to the physical map position.
- At 72 m and above, F29D remains the authoritative NATO HQ-counter system so F29I does not duplicate the full counter.
- A selected HQ is highlighted with the existing yellow/gold selection language.

### 2. Orders are visible on the battlefield
- Major and regimental HQs display their current order as a compact screen-space tag.
- The associated objective receives a matching objective tag, e.g. `MAJOR A • FORSVAR` or `REGIMENT • ANGRIB`.
- Order labels are shown for all HQs while Tactical Overlay is ON and always for the selected HQ even when the overlay is OFF.

### 3. Facing is readable
- A selected company receives a high-contrast facing arrow from its physical centre in the current formation direction.
- F29D continues to show facing on NATO counters at larger zoom levels.

### 4. Combat must be easy to find
- A company that is currently under fire or has recent hit feedback receives `UNDER ILD`.
- A company in an active mutual fire-contact geometry receives `KAMP`.
- Combat alerts use the existing red/orange danger language and are screen-space overlays, not simulation objects.

### 5. Tactical Overlay toggle
- `TAB` toggles the extended tactical layer.
- Essential close-zoom HQ discoverability remains available even when the extended overlay is OFF.
- Order/objective tags for unselected HQs and combat alerts are hidden when the overlay is OFF.
- Selected-unit facing remains visible because it is direct command feedback.

## Interaction with semantic zoom
F29I complements, rather than replaces, F29D.

- CLOSE: F29I close-HQ labels/beacons + selected facing + optional orders/combat.
- MEDIUM: F29D selected/NATO information + F29I order/objective/combat status.
- OPERATIONAL: F29D NATO counters are primary; F29I adds current intent and combat alerts.
- STRATEGIC: F29D remains responsible for 3D mesh suppression and NATO representation; F29I only adds tactical status annotations.

## Non-goals in F29I
The following remain later visibility work and are not part of this revision:
- battalion frontage polygons,
- command-range quality colouring,
- aggregation/deaggregation of company counters into battalion/regiment counters,
- dedicated hold-to-show Command View/OOB overlay,
- fog-of-war-aware intelligence confidence on tactical symbols.

## QA
1. At close zoom, Major A, Major B and the Oberstløjtnant are easy to find by fixed-size HQ labels/beacons.
2. At >=72 m, the existing F29D HQ NATO counters remain the primary HQ presentation without a duplicated F29I full HQ counter.
3. Give `FORSVAR HER`, `ANGRIB HER`, `RYK FREM`, `TILBAGETRÆK` or `SAML`; the HQ and objective must show the corresponding order text.
4. Select one company; a clear facing arrow must point in its current formation direction.
5. Exchange fire; involved formations must show `KAMP` / `UNDER ILD` without changing simulation state.
6. Press TAB; extended order/objective/combat information must toggle OFF/ON without affecting unit selection, AI or movement.
7. The bottom command HUD remains unobstructed.
