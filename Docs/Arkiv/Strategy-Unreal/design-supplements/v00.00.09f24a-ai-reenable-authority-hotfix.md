# v00.00.09f24a — AI re-enable authority hotfix

## Problem

A company could be detached from the Major with a direct player order (AI OFF), then when the player switched the company back to AI ON the battalion controller automatically restored the company's `lastAssignments` entry. That resurrected the previous Major destination, even when the company still had a valid player-issued route in the opposite direction.

Observed symptom: a manually redirected company was switched back to AI ON and immediately turned toward the old Major mission / bridge instead of continuing its current mission.

## Canonical authority rule

Turning **company AI ON** changes who executes the company's **current** mission; it must not silently change what that mission is.

- Direct player company order -> company detaches from Major, AI OFF.
- Player toggles that detached company AI ON -> company remains detached from the old Major mission and continues/executes its current player mission with local Captain AI.
- Old `lastAssignments` must never be restored merely because AI was toggled ON.
- Only a **new Major order** may reclaim the company into battalion authority and replace its current mission.
- A new Major order is still allowed to set subordinate company AI ON as part of reclaiming the battalion.

This keeps `AI ON/OFF` as an execution/delegation control, not a hidden order-history selector.

## QA

1. Major gives FORSVAR HER.
2. Select one subordinate company and give it a manual move in the opposite direction; AI becomes OFF and the Major mission is detached.
3. Toggle that company AI ON without issuing a new Major order.
4. The company must continue toward the manual destination; it must not resume the old Major slot or route toward the bridge.
5. Give the Major a NEW order.
6. The company may now be reclaimed, AI ON, and receive the new battalion assignment.
