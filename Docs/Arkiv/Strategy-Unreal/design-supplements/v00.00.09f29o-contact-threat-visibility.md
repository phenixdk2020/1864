# PROJECT 1864 — v00.00.09f29o Contact Threat Visibility

## Scope
F29O addresses two QA problems visible in the F29N battle test:

1. The player could not see nearby enemy fire cones while judging whether companies deployed/engaged at the correct range.
2. A company on an `ANGRIB HER` march could continue along navigation steering past an enemy on its flank instead of stopping, turning and engaging when that enemy entered the company's selected fire-policy range.

## Enemy threat cones
When at least one Danish company is selected, nearby live Prussian companies show their valid fire cones even though the enemy company itself is not selected.

Rules:
- only enemies within 180 m of a selected Danish company are previewed;
- cones are only shown if the enemy formation is actually fire-ready;
- enemy cones use a distinct red threat palette so they are visually separate from the selected friendly company's yellow/orange/red range bands;
- Column, reforming Line, active Charge and charge-created melee still suppress cones because those formations may not currently deliver normal volley fire;
- enemy threat preview is visual only and does not alter simulation or AI.

## Flank contact during attack
For a company executing a higher `AttackHere` mission:

- fire policy remains the maximum local contact trigger;
- enemy contact is detected by range, not by the company's current facing arc;
- therefore an enemy entering CLOSE/MED/LONG from the flank or rear can interrupt the march;
- the company immediately drops the parent march leg, deploys/stays in Line, turns toward the detected enemy and receives an explicit `AttackTarget` mission;
- the parent Major mission remains stored and may resume if the contact becomes invalid;
- navigation helpers may not keep steering the company toward an obsolete detour while local attack-contact authority is active.

## Navigation cleanup
The canonical battlefield rules already define Trees and FencePosts as pass-through scenery. F29O therefore removes Trees/FencePosts from the F29E wide-map hard-detour list. Farmhouse/Barn remain local hard endpoint/travel blockers.

This specifically prevents repeated `FencePost` detour replanning from forcing a fighting company to keep marching/entering Column while a nearby enemy should own its attention.

## QA
1. Select a Danish company near the enemy. Nearby Prussian fire cones must become visible without selecting the Prussian company.
2. Move the selected Danish company away beyond 180 m; unrelated enemy cones should disappear.
3. Enemy company in Column/reforming/Charge/melee must not show a normal firing cone.
4. Give `ANGRIB HER` with MED selected and let an enemy enter MED from the company's side. The company must stop the parent march, remain/deploy Line, turn and engage that enemy.
5. Repeat with LONG and CLOSE to verify the selected fire policy is still the trigger threshold.
6. Verify no repeated `NAV-WIDE...Blocker=FencePost` detours occur.
7. Regression: bridge-only navigation remains authoritative at the river.
8. Regression: F29N Charge remains Line/HOLD FIRE with hidden cone during charge/melee.
