# PROJECT 1864 – v00.00.09f8 range/cone/morale/turning test

## Status
TEST supplement for the current 190-man company battle prototype.

## Fire-distance tuning
The current tactical QA scale remains 1 Unity unit = 1 metre.

Current TEST bands:
- Close: 35 m
- Medium / Effective: 70 m
- Long / Maximum: 100 m

These values are deliberately shorter than v00.00.09f7 (40 / 80 / 115 m). The Long line is placed between the previous Medium and Long visual distances, while Close and Medium are reduced proportionally.

This is a gameplay/visual QA profile, not the final historical weapon database. Historical weapon types, effective ranges, training and doctrine remain future data-driven work.

## Fire-sector visual rule
The total fire sector remains 70 degrees (+/-35 degrees).

Close, Medium and Long fans must share the same left/right boundary directions. The shorter fans must not visually pinch inward relative to the Long fan. All three bands originate across the full firing frontage.

## Formation-ready firing
The range cone is hidden while the company is in Column.

When the company changes Column -> Line, the cone remains hidden until the physical reform animation is estimated to be complete. Firing is also blocked during this period, so the UI and combat model agree.

Design rule:
- Column: no range cone and no formed-line volley fire.
- Reforming to Line: no range cone and no formed-line volley fire.
- Line ready: range cone and normal fire policy available.

## Morale-off hit QA
Morale is temporarily locked at 100 in v00.00.09f8 so accumulated morale shock does not obscure raw casualty testing.

Distance remains active in the accuracy calculation. Short, Medium and Long therefore still produce different expected hit rates. Cohesion remains active in this QA revision.

Whenever casualties are registered, Console telemetry reports:
- attacker
- target
- distance
- SHORT / MEDIUM / LONG band
- hits/losses
- remaining strength
- cohesion

This is intended to make range lethality measurable before morale is re-enabled.

## Turning-speed tuning
Stationary formation facing changes are deliberately slower in v00.00.09f8.

- Previous stationary turn speed: 24 degrees/second
- v00.00.09f8 test speed: 16.8 degrees/second
- Change: 30% slower

This applies to stationary facing changes such as left/right facing orders. Normal movement steering remains owned by the movement system and is not reduced by this tuning.

## Retained rules
- 190-man company baseline.
- Danish 1851 Line baseline uses three ranks.
- Line <-> Column physical reforming remains visible.
- Normal walk, Forced March and routed Run remain active.
- AWAIT -> AIM -> FIRE -> RELOAD volley visuals remain active.
- Full-front black-powder smoke remains active.
- Fallen soldiers remain close to the actual formation slots where they fell.
- Selection/destination footprints continue to use exact formation bounds.
- River crossing remains bridge-only; scenery remains pass-through in the current navigation baseline.
