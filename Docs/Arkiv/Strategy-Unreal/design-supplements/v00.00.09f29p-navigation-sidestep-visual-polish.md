# PROJECT 1864 — v00.00.09f29p

## Tactical map + camera navigation + formation sidestep + visual polish

Status: **TEST**.

F29P improves battlefield readability and short-range formation movement without changing the core F29O contact rules, F29N charge rules or F29M Square rules.

## 1. Formation-preserving Side Step

A line formation must not rotate 90 degrees merely to move 10–12 metres sideways and then rotate back.

F29P adds a dedicated short lateral correction:

- a company detecting a friendly formation closer than about **13 m** may shift left/right;
- default lateral correction is about **12 m**;
- movement speed is deliberately slow, about **1.45 m/s**;
- the original facing is preserved for the full movement;
- the unit remains in its current formation; Side Step is not Column movement;
- only one formation in an overlapping pair is selected as the moving formation by a deterministic tie-break;
- the side with the best local friendly clearance is chosen;
- a candidate Side Step into the river corridor is rejected;
- the parent Major/regimental mission is temporarily suspended only while the short correction runs and may resume afterwards.

Side Step must not override higher priority tactical states:

- Square;
- bridge-only routing;
- local AttackContact;
- under-fire emergency reaction;
- Charge / charge-created melee;
- explicit forced target combat;
- immediate physical enemy proximity.

## 2. Tactical overview map

F29P adds a compact tactical map above the bottom command HUD.

The map uses dynamic local bounds around the active formations, while still respecting the full 5760 × 3840 m battlefield.

Visual language:

- Danish companies: blue;
- Prussian companies: red;
- selected company: yellow;
- Major HQ: cyan;
- Oberstløjtnant HQ: gold;
- road: brown;
- river: blue;
- camera position/direction: white.

The map is presentation/navigation only. It does not change simulation authority.

### Map / camera controls

- `M` — toggle tactical map.
- `F` — focus camera on selected company/HQ while retaining current tactical camera orientation/height.
- `Shift+F` — place camera behind the selected unit/HQ at close tactical zoom and align with its facing.
- `F1` — focus Major A.
- `F2` — focus Major B.
- `F3` — focus Oberstløjtnant.
- `N` — orient camera north.
- map UI buttons `<` / `>` rotate the camera 45 degrees around the selected formation.
- `FOCUS` performs normal selected focus.
- `BAG` performs behind-selected tactical view.

The existing WASD/arrow, Q/E and mouse-wheel camera controls remain unchanged.

## 3. Visual battlefield polish

F29P adds presentation-only elements with no colliders:

- a visible captain figure at each company centre;
- distinct Major officer + aide group;
- distinct Oberstløjtnant + two staff figures;
- small HQ ground discs to make officers easier to identify at tactical zoom;
- subtle field-boundary lines around several central battlefield fields to break up the very large plain grass surface and improve perception of distance/position.

The existing F7 active soldier renderer remains authoritative for rank-and-file soldiers, movement poses, volley poses, casualties and smoke. Existing F4 national/regimental standards remain authoritative for flags.

## 4. Future captain tactical-opportunity doctrine

F29P intentionally does **not yet** make every captain autonomously leave his mission to flank an already engaged enemy. The physical Side Step foundation is introduced first.

The planned model is now recorded in `docs/backlog/B-280-OFFICER-PERSONALITY-ORDER-COMPLIANCE.md`:

- a good captain uses Tactical Skill, Initiative, Experience and Composure to identify a safe opportunity against an enemy already occupied by another friendly unit;
- he may use Side Step / Oblique movement to obtain free frontage and enter the chosen firing range;
- he should reject the opportunity if another hostile formation can gain dangerous flank/rear fire on him;
- a weaker or over-aggressive captain can misread that safety assessment and attempt the manoeuvre anyway;
- bad decisions must come from officer stats + decision noise, not from a hard rule that a poor officer always behaves irrationally;
- the parent Major/regimental mission is retained and resumes when the local opportunity ends.
