# v00.00.09f24 — Major mission/destination authority

## Status
TEST implementation supplement.

## Purpose
This revision closes three related authority/visualisation defects observed during battalion testing:

1. a subordinate company could no longer see its Major-assigned destination after selection moved from the Major to the company;
2. a newly issued Major order could coexist with stale execution from the previous order;
3. companies could stop several metres short of the displayed destination because enemy fire-range proximity was treated as mission arrival.

## Canonical rules introduced

### 1. Destination visibility follows ownership and selection
A company that is still owned by the Major mission keeps that mission when selected directly.

Selecting a company alone does **not** mean manual control.

Therefore:
- Major selected: show the battalion command links and all active subordinate mission destinations;
- one or more Major-owned companies selected: show the active destination/path for each selected company;
- direct manual company order: company becomes AI OFF/manual and its Major mission visual is removed.

### 2. A new Major order is a hard replacement
A new Major command is not an appended waypoint and is not advisory.

When the Major receives a new explicit order:
- stale PlayerCommander routes are cleared;
- stale manual destination ghosts are cleared with those routes;
- any old direct attack target is cleared;
- old local Officer-AI intent is flushed;
- the new Major mission set becomes authoritative immediately.

This also applies if the player repeats the same order at the same map point. A fresh mission set is still a fresh command event.

### 3. Displayed company slot is a commitment
A Major destination footprint is a promise about where the company is intended to finish its manoeuvre.

A company may therefore not set `Arrived=True` merely because an enemy enters CLOSE/MEDIUM/LONG range before the slot is reached.

Current QA arrival tolerance:
- approximately **4.5 m** from the assigned company slot.

During the approach:
- `AIEnabled` remains ON;
- local Officer-AI decision updates may be paused temporarily so the subordinate does not abandon the assigned manoeuvre;
- bridge/building steering still has terrain authority and may temporarily replace the strategic goal with a legal intermediate waypoint;
- the strategic goal must not overwrite a live legal terrain-routing waypoint.

After reaching the assigned slot:
- local Officer AI is released again;
- the company may resume doctrine-based local combat behaviour within the Major's mission intent.

## Authority hierarchy clarification
For a Major-owned company in transit:

1. rout/panic;
2. hard terrain legality / bridge routing / obstacle steering;
3. direct player manual company order;
4. current Major mission and assigned company slot;
5. subordinate Officer-AI local initiative;
6. generic formation/fire behaviour.

A lower level may not silently cancel a higher-level destination.

## QA scenarios
1. Give `FORSVAR HER`, select one subordinate company, and verify its Major path/box remains visible.
2. Select multiple subordinate companies without giving a direct manual order; each must show its own Major destination.
3. Issue a second `FORSVAR HER` before the first completes; old movement must be replaced.
4. Repeat the same `FORSVAR HER` point; the command must still be treated as a fresh replacement.
5. Put an enemy inside fire range while the company is still 10-30 m from its assigned slot; the company must continue to the slot rather than declaring arrival early.
6. Repeat near Farmhouse/Barn and across the river; terrain routing must remain authoritative while the final slot remains the strategic destination.
