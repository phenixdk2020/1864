# v00.00.09f18 – Unified officer AI and command authority

Status: **ACTIVE PROTOTYPE RULESET**

This supplement defines the command-authority model introduced in v00.00.09f18. It supersedes the temporary 09f17 HQ authority behavior where Major missions, company manual routes and separate HQ/unit AI controls could compete.

## 1. One AI concept at every echelon

The UI and gameplay use one common concept: **AI ON / AI OFF**.

- Company/Kaptajn AI uses `OfficerAIController`.
- Major AI uses the battalion-level decision layer.
- Both use the same UI language and doctrine choices: **DEF / BAL / OFF**.
- `AI ON` means the officer is allowed to interpret and execute assigned intent.
- `AI OFF` means direct player control has authority.

Future Kaptajn, Major, Oberstløjtnant, Oberst and Generalmajor implementations must retain this same authority vocabulary.

## 2. Manual company order rule

A direct manual company order has priority over the current Major mission.

When the player directly commands a company with movement/hold/formation/facing controls:

1. Company AI is switched **OFF before PlayerCommander consumes the order**.
2. The company is detached from the current Major mission.
3. The existing Major mission may not silently reclaim it.
4. The company remains under direct player control until either:
   - the player manually switches that company AI back **ON**, or
   - the Major receives a **new order**.

If company AI is manually switched ON and a previous Major assignment still exists, the company rejoins that assignment.

## 3. New Major order rule

A new explicit Major order is a new command-authority event.

It:

- clears all subordinate manual-detachment flags;
- clears stale `PlayerCommander` movement routes and their destination ghosts;
- switches the subordinate companies to **AI ON**;
- creates fresh Major-owned mission destinations;
- retains authority until the mission is completed or the player manually detaches a company again.

This prevents the 09f17 failure where companies completed the Major order and then walked back toward old player destinations.

## 4. Major selection and movement

The physical Major/HQ is a selectable battlefield entity.

- Normal click can select the Major.
- The standard left-mouse drag rectangle can also select the Major.
- Selecting the Major clears company selection and replaces the company bottom bar with the Major panel.
- The Major may be moved with right-click ground while selected.
- A direct manual Major move switches **Major AI OFF**, following the same authority rule as a company.

Later, a higher-echelon order may switch the Major back to AI ON in the same way a new Major order currently reclaims companies.

## 5. Major order visibility

When the Major is selected:

- command-links are drawn between Major and subordinate companies;
- each active Major mission has a path line;
- each company has its own final formation footprint;
- old manual destination ghosts are cleared when the Major takes authority.

This is separate from the future courier/order-delay visualization. The courier will later travel along the command-link and represent delivery progress.

## 6. Attack/defend execution

Major orders allocate separate company slots instead of sending every company toward one shared target transform.

For `ANGRIB HER` and `FORSVAR HER`:

- companies receive side-by-side formation goals;
- the current 60 m centre spacing remains the company-level baseline;
- an attacking battalion normally forms approximately 68 m from the nearest known threat when a threat can be resolved;
- a company stops manoeuvring and forms Line when it reaches its assigned slot **or** a valid enemy enters its selected fire-policy range;
- it then holds and fires instead of continuing to mill around under fire.

Manual multi-company attack-lane protection from 09f17 remains relevant for direct player attacks outside the Major system.

## 7. Safe final formation positions

Major assignment endpoints are checked before use.

Current hard endpoint checks include:

- Farmhouse formation footprint;
- Barn formation footprint;
- open river positions away from the bridge;
- battlefield bounds.

The existing formation-level building/bridge routing remains responsible for finding the legal path to the chosen endpoint.

## 8. Reserve is a decision, not a fixed template

A Major is **not required** to create a reserve.

Current prototype decision baseline:

- only one known enemy threat: normally all available companies go into the primary front;
- multiple known threats: a reserve may be considered;
- a reserve requires four active companies plus adequate battalion strength and cohesion;
- the freshest/cohesive company is preferred when a reserve is justified;
- weak battalions generally mass the remaining strength instead of automatically withholding a reserve.

The exact thresholds are prototype values and will later be driven by officer stats, doctrine, terrain and enemy intelligence.

## 9. Flanking is conditional

A reserve does not automatically flank.

A flank is only attempted when:

- a reserve exists;
- battalion condition is adequate;
- the known enemy disposition leaves one lateral side materially less occupied;
- the candidate flank endpoint is legal.

If both sides are similarly occupied, no automatic flank is selected.

An arrived reserve can later be committed toward a heavily threatened front company.

## 10. Known enemy information

09f18 uses a temporary prototype knowledge model: enemies within roughly **700 m** of the Major or subordinate companies are treated as known, with a shorter local fallback around the current objective.

This is **not final fog-of-war**. Later versions must replace it with:

- LOS/observation;
- scouting;
- reports from subordinate officers;
- delayed information;
- courier delivery;
- officer/staff quality;
- stale/incorrect battlefield knowledge.

Reserve and flanking decisions must eventually operate only on information actually available to the Major.

## 11. Future officer personality

The command-authority model remains compatible with the planned officer personality system.

A future officer can have AI ON and still interpret intent differently according to Initiative, Discipline/Obedience, Aggressiveness/Caution, Tactical Skill, Composure, Leadership and Experience. `AI ON` therefore means the officer has discretion; it does not mean perfect obedience.
