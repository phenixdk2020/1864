## 1. Tactical map model

The tactical map is a fixed overview of the complete tactical battlefield. Its geographic bounds do not recalculate around currently active companies.

The camera is represented inside the fixed map by a moving white viewport/position marker. Units therefore move inside one stable map reference instead of the minimap itself appearing to pan/zoom around the units.

Map symbols:
- Danish company: blue marker.
- Enemy company: red marker.
- Selected company: yellow marker.
- Major HQ: explicit `II` HQ marker.
- Regimental/Oberstløjtnant HQ: explicit `III` HQ marker.
- Crop fields: yellow field outlines.
- River: blue line.
- Road: brown line.
- Camera viewport/position: white.

Interaction follows the OOB rule:
- single-click own company/HQ = change command selection only; camera remains where the battle is being observed,
- double-click same own company/HQ = selection + camera behind the entity,
- click empty map or enemy marker area = move camera to that map area without selecting the enemy.

## 2. Semantic zoom handoff

F29V owns the semantic NATO handoff to avoid the previous F29D/F29Q overlap.

Thresholds:
- HQ NATO identification: from 72 m,
- all company NATO counters: from 175 m,
- 175–314 m: physical 3D formations and NATO counters coexist,
- 315 m and above: physical unit/HQ meshes are hidden while NATO counters remain visible.

There must be no blank transition near 235 m. Company NATO counters remain present continuously once the 175 m threshold has been crossed.

NATO echelon notation remains:
- `I` = company,
- `II` = battalion/Major HQ,
- `III` = regiment/Oberstløjtnant HQ.

## 3. OOB tactical status

The OOB right column is not merely a generic `HOLD` label. It is a compact tactical-state summary.

Supported company states in F29V:
- `KLAR`
- `RYKKER`
- `FORSVAR`
- `RESERVE`
- `FLANKE`
- `SQUARE`
- `KAMP`
- `UNDER ILD`
- `CHARGE`
- `MELEE`
- `ROUT`

Priority is combat/survival state first, then mission role, then movement/readiness.

`FORSVAR` means the company is assigned to/has arrived in a defensive mission. F29V does **not** invent an entrenchment combat modifier. `GRAVET NED` is reserved for a future explicit entrenchment mechanic.

## 4. Square visual ownership

The actual square perimeter is the canonical Square formation outline and must remain visible when the selected company is in Square.

The long/thin Major/HQ mission `Footprint` rectangle is a destination/formation footprint for ordinary line deployment. It is hidden while the company is in Square because it no longer describes the active physical formation.

While Square is active:
- keep `SquareOutline`,
- suppress ordinary Line forward range fans,
- suppress legacy Square 360-degree Close/Medium/Long rings,
- show four directional Square fire sectors when the selected Square is ready and range display is enabled.

## 5. Square fire sectors

Square has four fixed 90-degree firing faces:
- FRONT,
- RIGHT,
- REAR,
- LEFT.

Each face has its own target selection and reload clock. Existing Square accuracy scaling continues to represent approximately one quarter of company fire contribution per face.

A face may fire only when:
1. the Square is ready,
2. fire policy is not Hold Fire,
3. an active enemy exists on that face,
4. the enemy is inside the currently selected fire-policy range,
5. `Regiment.CanFireAt` passes as the final volley gate.

No valid enemy means no new Square volley and no new volley smoke.

The old F29S sector owner is disabled when the F29V owner is present so two systems cannot issue Square volleys concurrently.

## 6. Authority / regression rules

F29V is UI/presentation/fire-gating work only. It must not become a normal movement owner.

Unchanged authority priorities include:
1. river/bridge hard safety,
2. charge/melee,
3. Square local physical formation state,
4. under-fire/local contact,
5. higher officer mission,
6. ordinary navigation.

No F29V change may bypass Kaptajn → Major → Oberstløjtnant command authority.
