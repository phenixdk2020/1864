# v00.00.09f21 – Manual attack authority isolation

## Problem
A directly player-controlled Danish company could fire one volley at the selected enemy and then turn/march toward an unrelated attack-lane position. The legacy 09f17 attack-lane layer grouped every Danish company that happened to have the same forced target, including Major/AI-controlled companies and manually detached companies.

## Rule
Direct manual company attack authority must be isolated from Major AI authority.

- A single manual company (Officer AI OFF) keeps its designated target and must not be absorbed into a Major/group attack lane.
- Major-controlled companies (Officer AI ON) are excluded from the manual attack-lane planner.
- Manual attack-lanes are only eligible when two or more currently player-selected Danish companies are all under direct manual control (Officer AI OFF) and share the same designated target.
- Major-owned attack placement remains owned by PrototypeMajorBattalion09F18 and its mission slots.
- River/bridge and hard-building route authority remain higher-priority legality layers and are not bypassed by this change.

## Expected result
A player may detach one company from the Major, right-click a specific enemy and keep that target. The company may manoeuvre as required by legal terrain routing, but it must not suddenly join another company's/Major's frontage plan or march to an unrelated lane.

## QA
1. Major AI ON with several companies fighting the same enemy.
2. Select only one Danish company and manually attack that enemy.
3. The selected company AI changes to OFF and leaves the Major mission.
4. It must retain the player-designated enemy rather than receiving an 09f17 shared attack-lane OrderMove.
5. Verify it can fire repeatedly when the target remains in range/arc.
6. Repeat with two manually selected AI-OFF companies attacking the same enemy; the manual multi-company lane planner may still separate them side-by-side.
7. Recheck bridge-only crossing and Farmhouse/Barn detours.
