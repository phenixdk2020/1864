# PROJECT 1864 — v00.00.09f29n

## Charge melee + HQ spacing hardening

Status: TEST design supplement.

### CHARGE — formation and fire authority

- An explicit infantry CHARGE is a bayonet-assault state, not ordinary march movement.
- The charging company remains in **Line** throughout the charge.
- Automatic march-column policy and bridge-column policy may steer the route, but may not reform an actively charging company into Column.
- The charging company is locked to **HOLD FIRE** for the complete charge approach and physical melee phase.
- Its previous fire policy is restored only when the charge ends.
- Fire cones/range fans are hidden while a company is actively charging.
- Once charge contact becomes physical melee, fire cones are hidden for both melee participants and musket volleys are suppressed while bayonet contact persists.

### CHARGE — physical contact

- The old ~7 m root-to-root stopping distance produced an obvious visual gap between formations.
- Explicit charge contact now continues to approximately **2.2 m root-to-root**, allowing the two company footprints to overlap deeply enough to read as actual close combat.
- Normal non-charge melee can still use the broader collider/contact envelope.
- Once deep charge contact is reached, the melee resolver owns losses, morale/cohesion shock, facing and hold state.

### HQ follow spacing

The F29L follow distances were visually too detached at tactical zoom.

- Major HQ target distance behind battalion centre: **105 m -> 80 m** (~24% closer).
- Major follow refresh threshold: **55 m -> 42 m**.
- Oberstløjtnant target distance behind midpoint of both Majors: **210 m -> 160 m** (~24% closer).
- Regimental follow refresh threshold: **90 m -> 68 m**.
- Manual HQ positioning, safe-relocation checks and mission authority remain unchanged.
