# PROJECT 1864 — v00.00.09f29k Charge Targeting + Input Isolation

## Purpose
F29K keeps the proven F25 infantry charge/melee mechanics but replaces the legacy left-click target picker with a clearer RTS-style armed command workflow. The same revision also fixes the known conflict where Major/Oberstløjtnant click-drag facing orders simultaneously created the blue `SELECT` marquee.

## Player workflow — CHARGE
1. Select one or more Danish companies.
2. Click **CHARGE** in the company HUD, or press `V`.
3. CHARGE immediately becomes visibly **green / armed**.
4. Hover a valid enemy formation; it is labelled **CHARGE MÅL**.
5. **Right-click** the enemy formation to confirm the charge.
6. `ESC` cancels. Clicking CHARGE/V again while armed also cancels.
7. A normal left-click never confirms charge; it returns to normal selection behaviour.

## Input ownership
While CHARGE is armed, right mouse input belongs exclusively to charge targeting. The same RMB event must not also become a PlayerCommander move/attack order. F29K therefore consumes the right-click edge before the ordinary player-order layer executes.

While a Major/Oberstløjtnant point-order is pending, left mouse input belongs exclusively to `PrototypeOfficerFacingOrder09F29G`. `PrototypeBoxSelection09H` must not start, draw or commit a selection rectangle until that officer-order transaction has ended.

This follows the project-wide rule: **one input owner and one physical movement owner at a time**.

## Fire and movement behaviour
F29K does not replace the F25 charge engine. After target confirmation F25 still:
- releases the company from higher/manual movement ownership,
- disables local AI authority for the charge,
- clears the normal route,
- stores the previous fire policy,
- sets fire policy to **HOLD FIRE**,
- reforms to line where possible,
- closes to physical contact,
- hands contact resolution to `PrototypeMeleeCombatManager`,
- restores the previous fire policy when the charge ends.

Therefore infantry must **not fire while charging toward the target**. A shot during an active charge is a bug/regression.

## Visual state
- normal: CHARGE button red/inactive;
- armed target-pick: CHARGE button green + `CHARGE ARMED — HØJREKLIK PÅ FJENDTLIG ENHED`;
- valid hovered enemy: `CHARGE MÅL`;
- active charge: existing F25 `IsCharging()` state keeps the normal HUD charge status active;
- officer point-order drag: only objective circle/facing arrow is shown — no blue `SELECT` box.

## QA
1. Unity compiles without red errors and visible marker reads `PROJECT 1864 | v00.00.09f29k TEST`.
2. Select a company and click CHARGE; button must immediately appear green before a target is chosen.
3. Left-clicking an enemy must not start a charge.
4. Right-clicking a valid enemy must start the charge and must not simultaneously create a normal move order.
5. Right-clicking empty terrain while armed must not issue a move order; charge remains armed.
6. ESC cancels armed target-pick.
7. Clicking CHARGE/V again while armed cancels target-pick.
8. During the charge the company must hold fire all the way to contact.
9. At contact melee takes over; after charge termination the previous HOLD/CLOSE/MED/LONG fire policy is restored.
10. Multi-selection: all armed valid selected companies charge the same confirmed target.
11. Select Major or Oberstløjtnant, click `FORSVAR HER`/another point-order and drag facing: the blue `SELECT` marquee must never appear and selection must remain unchanged.
