# v00.00.09f30m — Higher command delegation, temporary cavalry attachment & mission visual parity

## Higher command AI cascade
Division AI ON cascades:
Division -> Brigade -> Regiment -> Major A/B -> company Officer AI, plus cavalry Officer AI.

Brigade AI ON cascades:
Brigade -> Regiment -> Major A/B -> company Officer AI, plus cavalry Officer AI.

Direct player orders remain higher authority than inherited AI.

## Mission visualization parity
Selecting Brigade or Division reveals:
- company routes;
- destination footprints;
- regimental objective circle/cross;
- cavalry route and destination ghost;
- higher-level objective label.

These are views of the existing authoritative lower mission state, not duplicate route writers.

## Temporary cavalry task attachment during attack
Permanent and tactical command relationships are separate:

- OrganicParent = permanent OOB ownership.
- CurrentCommandParent = HQ currently exercising tactical command.

When a higher HQ with cavalry attached orders ANGRIB HER:
1. available cavalry is temporarily task-attached to attacking Battalion/Major HQs;
2. with two cavalry units and two battalions, allocation uses the lower total travel-cost pairing to reduce crossing;
3. cavalry executes support/flank/rear behavior under the temporary Battalion/Major parent;
4. OrganicParent is not changed.

When the infantry attack mission has no more executing company missions:
- a cavalry unit already in a committed CHARGE may finish;
- temporary attachment is released;
- CurrentCommandParent returns to the previous higher parent;
- attachment type becomes RESERVE;
- cavalry receives a reserve/assemble position behind and offset from that HQ.

A new non-attack higher mission also releases the temporary attack attachment immediately.

## Defend and other higher missions
FORSVAR HER gives attached cavalry separated reserve/support slots behind the objective.
Advance/withdraw/assemble use corresponding support staging goals.

## Active-order state
Higher mission buttons are blue while pending or while Regiment/Cavalry still have active executors. Arrived company missions no longer count as still executing.

## HUD parity
Brigade/Division use:
- ENHEDSINFO / AI
- ORDRER / MISSION
- UNDERLAGTE / STATUS / ATTACHMENT

The right side shows subordinate AI state so delegation can be verified visually.
