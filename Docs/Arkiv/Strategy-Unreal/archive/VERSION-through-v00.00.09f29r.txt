v00.00.09f29r - Crop field concealment + map polish
- Updates the canonical Markdown design manual first to designbaseline v00.02.15 and makes F29R the current terrain/readability baseline.
- Converts the six F29P field-boundary areas into shared crop-field gameplay geometry with center/width/depth/yaw/density data.
- Adds yellow/golden field base surfaces plus low crop rows that partially hide infantry legs/lower silhouettes at close zoom.
- Crop visuals have no colliders and do not become navigation blockers.
- Dense crop scales movement to about x0.92 after the existing F7 movement-mode speed writer; F29R does not own route or destination.
- Targets inside crop receive concealment rather than ballistic cover: roughly -2% hit chance at CLOSE, -5% around MEDIUM and -10% toward LONG, density-scaled.
- Long-range target acquisition is shortened by the crop visibility factor; a ready volley is briefly delayed while the target remains beyond the concealed acquisition threshold.
- A unit firing from crop is temporarily revealed for about 4.5 seconds by activity/black-powder smoke, reducing the concealment effect.
- F14 close-range lethality supplement now uses the same F29R terrain multiplier and logs Terrain=x..., so the legacy 16-hit cap cannot erase concealment tuning.
- Adds yellow crop-field overlays to the existing F29P tactical map using the same gameplay field data; M still governs map visibility.
- F29Q OOB/HQ navigation, NATO semantic zoom, F29P Side Step/camera, F29O contact/cones, F29N Charge and F29M Square remain unchanged.
- Full crop trampling/deformation, cavalry-specific crop handling and officer-stat-driven use of concealment remain future work.
- Backup: backup/channel-test-v00.00.09f29q-pre-09f29r-crop-fields.
